# Hi, I'm Marianne. 👋

I'm a senior product designer + builder working across **product strategy, systems, UX, and AI-assisted product development**.

I gravitate toward complicated problems — interconnected workflows, business rules, customer behavior, operational constraints, and ideas that aren't fully formed yet — and turn them into experiences and systems people can understand, use, and build.

## What I'm exploring

The line between **designing products and building products** is changing.

I'm using GitHub to document that part of my practice: experimenting with AI-assisted development, building products of my own, and developing better ways to move from:

**ambiguity → structure → experience → implementation**

You'll find work here around:

* Product and experience strategy
* Complex workflows and systems
* Product documentation and decision-making
* Design-to-development workflows
* AI-assisted product development
* Reusable frameworks and experiments
